# Adkar data source

The bundled Arabic text and English meanings are selected from **Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim)** by Sa'eed bin Ali bin Wahf al-Qahtani, via the [Islamic Pro — Azkar API](https://github.com/YousefAsalya/Islamic-Pro-azkar-API). The source data is provided under the MIT License; see [LICENSE](./LICENSE).

`daily.json` contains the source's morning-and-evening category, shown in both the Day / Morning and Evening sections, plus its before-sleep, night-waking, sleep-fright, and bad-dream categories in the Night section. Repeat counts are retained from the source. Audio files and unrelated categories are not bundled.
